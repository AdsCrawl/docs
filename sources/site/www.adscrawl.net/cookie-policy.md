# Cookie Policy

How AdsCrawl uses cookies and similar technologies, what they are used for, and how you can control your choices.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They can help a website recognize a session or remember a choice when you return. Session cookies generally last until your browsing session ends, while persistent cookies have an expiry date.

Similar technologies, including local storage, can also remember information on your device. This policy covers these technologies on the AdsCrawl public website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

[Privacy Policy](https://www.adscrawl.net/privacy-policy/)

## Essential storage and preferences

Essential storage supports requested website functions and remembers your privacy choices. Selecting Necessary only does not disable this storage.

- Your Cookie choice: adscrawl-cookie-consent is stored in this website's local storage. It records whether you allowed optional services, the policy version, and the time of your choice. We ask again after 180 days, if you clear this record, or if its version is no longer valid.
- Language preference: adscrawl-locale remembers a language you select. It remains in local storage until you change it or clear your website data.
- Authentication and security: when you sign in to AdsCrawl, the account and API services use their own session cookies. Rejecting optional website services does not sign you out. Blocking essential session cookies in your browser may prevent sign-in or other account features from working.

## Optional analytics and live chat

We load the following optional services only after you select Accept all. Choosing Necessary only keeps their scripts from loading on subsequent page visits.

- Website analytics: where configured, Umami helps us understand page visits and website usage. We require your consent before loading it, including configurations that do not use cookies. Data handling depends on the analytics deployment.
- Live chat: SaleSmartly provides the website's support widget. Its service may use cookies or similar storage to maintain conversations and recognize a chat session. If you reject optional services, this widget is not loaded.
- These providers may receive browser and device information when their scripts load, and may manage their own storage and retention. This website's 180-day consent period is not a retention period for provider data.

[About Umami](https://umami.is/) [About SaleSmartly](https://www.salesmartly.com/)

## Your choices

The homepage notice lets you accept optional analytics and live chat or continue with necessary storage only. Your choice applies to this website in the browser and device where you made it. It does not automatically apply to other devices or to cookies inside a Cloud Browser session.

You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings below. This page is also linked from our Privacy Policy. Withdrawing an earlier acceptance reloads the page to stop scripts that were already running. It does not undo processing that occurred before withdrawal or automatically remove storage previously set by third-party providers.

If your browser prevents us from saving your choice, we display a message and do not enable optional services through that action. Enable website storage to save a preference and try again.

## Managing browser storage

Your browser settings let you view, block, or delete cookies and other website data. Clearing cookies alone may not remove local storage; use the browser's website-data controls to remove both. Clearing the consent record makes the homepage ask for your choice again.

Blocking all cookies or site storage may affect language preferences, sign-in, and other requested features. Storage set by a third-party provider may also need to be cleared for that provider's domain.

[Chrome storage controls](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647) [Firefox cookie controls](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)

We may update this policy as our website and service providers change. The date above identifies the latest update. Material changes to consent-controlled services may require a new choice before those services are enabled.
