# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information AdsCrawl collects, why we use it, who may process it, and how you can ask us to access, correct, or delete it.

Last updated: May 9, 2026

## Information We Collect

- Account information such as email address, authentication identifiers, and basic profile details.
- Billing and plan information handled directly or through payment providers.
- Usage data such as API requests, browser sessions, logs, errors, device data, and security events.
- Support messages, feedback, and any content you choose to submit while using the service.

## How We Use Information

- Provide, secure, operate, troubleshoot, and improve AdsCrawl.
- Authenticate users, issue API keys, prevent abuse, and enforce service limits.
- Process payments, send service notices, respond to support requests, and communicate product updates.
- Analyze reliability, performance, and feature usage in aggregated or de-identified ways where practical.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, language preferences, security, and product analytics. You can control cookies through your browser, but some parts of the service may not work correctly without essential cookies.

[Cookie Policy](https://www.adscrawl.net/cookie-policy/)

## Sharing and Processors

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us host, authenticate, process payments, deliver email, analyze product reliability, and provide support. These providers may process information only for the services they provide to us.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

## Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including access controls and encryption in transit. No service can guarantee perfect security.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security logs.

## Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You can contact support to make a request.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain privacy requests.

## International Transfers and Children

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the service is not intended for children.

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the product, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
